# ARGUS SOURCE QUALITY SCORING

Score sources from 0 to 10.

10 — official docs, program policy, accepted reports with clear evidence, judged contest findings, exploit postmortems with proof
8 — reputable research blog with PoC, high-quality audit writeup, strong technical breakdown
7 — good practical writeup with reproducible method
6 — labs, CTF material, useful but artificial
4 — generic blog post without reproduction
2 — social media claim, vague tip, no proof
0 — AI-generated spam, copied listicle, outdated or unverifiable claim

Rules:

- Source quality 8–10 may update mature skills if the extracted lesson is specific and actionable.
- Source quality 5–7 creates a skill patch proposal.
- Source quality 0–4 goes to inbox, rejected lessons, or requires later validation.
- Accepted reports, rejected reports, duplicates, and triager feedback have higher learning value than generic articles.

AppSec.fyi handling:

- AppSec.fyi root and topic pages are curated indexes, not final authorities.
- AppSec.fyi topic pages should create learning candidates.
- Linked original articles should be scored independently.
- AppSec.fyi topic page itself defaults to 6/10 unless it contains unique methodology.
